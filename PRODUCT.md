# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One authenticated construction site visitor records daily progress on a phone in Australia. An employer receives the exported PDF.

## Product Purpose

Capture live photos with fresh GPS, review concise English descriptions, and save one professional PDF for a selected day.

## Operating Context

Use outdoors on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, including when the connection drops. Today and capture-time display follow the device's local clock. Photo notes may be in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

## Capabilities and Constraints

A private single-user diary with date history, mandatory geotags, local drafts and foreground sync. The operator can write descriptions, edit the report from the preview, and download or share it. Supabase stores the cloud copy in Sydney. Keep labour, materials and cost tracking outside the diary.
